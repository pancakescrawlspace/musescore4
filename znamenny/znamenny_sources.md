# Znamenny chant: sources

Research of 2026-10-23. The questions: how is Znamenny chant (*знаменный
распев*) related to the hymns in this repository, and where can it be
found in good printed or typeset copies, in staff notation rather than
hook notation (*kryuki*)?

## Relation to this repository

None of the hymns identified in the other `*_sources.md` files is in
Znamenny chant. They belong to the later, simpler chants and to
19th-century common practice:

| piece | chant | file |
|---|---|---|
| Heer, ik roep, tones 1–8 | Moscow obikhod (Court chant; Kievan in tones 2 and 7) | `docs/heer_ik_roep_sources.md` |
| Bogoroditse Devo / Verheug U Maagd | Greek chant, arr. Arkhangelsky | `docs/bogoroditse_dievo_sources.md` |
| Vriendelijk licht | Kievan chant, arr. Dvoretsky | `docs/vriendelijk_licht_sources.md` |
| Welzalig is de mens | common obikhod («Обычный напев») | `docs/blazhen_muzh_sources.md` |
| Eucharistische Kanon (liturgy) | Feofan's own composition | `docs/milost_mira_sources.md` |
| In waarheid (liturgy) | parish "Kievan" «Достойно есть» | `docs/dostojno_est_sources.md` |

The following is general background, not checked against a source here.
Znamenny chant is the oldest Russian chant: monophonic, and written in
neumes (*znamena*) until the 17th century. The Kievan, Greek and
Bulgarian chants of the 17th century, and the harmonized obikhod of the
19th, are later layers.

Where Znamenny chant meets this repertoire is in the **texts**. The
editions below set many of the texts the choir sings (Господи воззвах,
Свете тихий, Блажен муж, Богородице Дево, Милость мира, Достойно есть)
in Znamenny chant, so the two versions of a text can be compared. Znamenny
settings came up as candidates during the research (e.g. for «Блажен
муж»), but none matched the choir's versions.

The hymn that prompted this was «Царю Небесный», which is on the
[Wikipedia page on Znamenny chant](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Znamenny_chant),
sung by the male chamber choir «Древнерусский распев» of the Moscow
Patriarchate. It is not in the repository. It is not in the 1909
Obikhod either (both tables of contents checked), but it is in the
Synodal Triodion; see "«Царю Небесный»" below.

## «Царю Небесный»

**Found in the Synodal *Триодь нотного пения*, part 2 («Цветная»,
the Pentecostarion; 1899 according to the library's listing), p. 311:**
«По 50-м псалме, стихира, глас 6: Царю небесный, Утешителю, Душе
истины…», the sticheron after Psalm 50 at the Matins of Pentecost. This
is the text sung on the Wikipedia recording. Whether the recording
follows this version, or the hook books directly, has not been checked.

- The PDF is from the "Глаголь" library
  ([Цветная](http://quadratum.glagol-verbum.com/biblioteka/triod_col.pdf); the Lenten part is
  [triod_lent.pdf](http://quadratum.glagol-verbum.com/biblioteka/triod_lent.pdf)). It is a 2004 retypesetting
  (PageMaker, like the Liturgy part of the 1909 Obikhod) in square
  notation, 127 pages, with the original page numbers (205–330; PDF page
  = printed page − 203). The pages for Pentecost are 304–322; the
  sticheron is on PDF p. 108.
- The Synodal Triodion has no table of contents. Pentecost Vespers
  (pp. 304–310) prints the stichera on «Господи воззвах», the litiya and
  the aposticha, but «Царю Небесный» only appears at Matins.
- The book is in square notation. That its chant is Znamenny is general
  knowledge about the Synodal Triodion, not stated on the page.
- In staff notation, azbyka.ru has only harmonized versions
  ([page](https://azbyka.ru/kliros/pyatidesyatnicza-troicza-noty-stihiry-czaryu-nebesnyj/)):
  «Малый знаменный напев» (four voices) and Znamenny harmonizations by
  Azeev, Solov'ev and Filipsonov. No unison staff-notation copy was
  found.

## Transcription: `svete_tihij_znamennyj.mscz`

In this folder: the Znamenny «Свете тихий» of azbyka.ru's Vespers series,
no. 09_1 («Свете Тихий, знаменный распев»,
[PDF](https://azbyka.ru/kliros/wp-content/uploads/2025/04/09_1_svete-tihij.pdf);
`references/znamenny/azbyka_series/09_1_svete-tihij.pdf`), transcribed
on 2026-10-23.

- **The notes** were read from the PDF's vector shapes (it has no music
  font): 9×6 filled heads are quarters, 9×6 hollow heads halves, 11×7
  hollow heads wholes, plus one dotted half. That gives 128 notes. Each
  pitch comes from the head's position on its staff (treble clef, one
  flat).
- **The syllables** (95) were assigned to the notes by their positions in
  the PDF's text layer. The multi-note syllables are exactly the source's
  slurred groups. The text is in civil script with the source's syllable
  division (e.g. «без-сме-ртна-го», «со-лнца»).
- **House style:** one measure per printed line with hidden time
  signatures (no courtesy signatures), slurs and melisma lines on all 24
  multi-note syllables (words split after a melisma), lyrics in Arial 14
  with minimum distance 1.3.
- **Outputs:** `svete_tihij_znamennyj.musicxml`, `.ly` and `.pdf` in this
  folder, built with `tools/rebuild-score.py znamenny/svete_tihij_znamennyj`.
  The note check is skipped: `tools/check-pdf-notes.py` handles only the
  two-staff choir layout.
- **Corrected on 2026-10-24:** «петь бы-ти» was a misreading of the
  source's «пѣ́тъ бы́ти» (the short participle «пет», not the infinitive);
  it now reads «пет».
- **Not done yet:** Kutuzov's tone 6. (The series' tones 2, 3 and 6 are
  done, see the next section.)

## Transcriptions: «Свете тихий», tones 2, 3, 6, and «Богородице Дево»

In this folder, transcribed on 2026-10-24 from the same Vespers series
(`references/znamenny/azbyka_series/`):

| score | source | notes | syllables |
|---|---|---|---|
| `svete_tihij_glas_2_znamennyj` | 09_2 «Свете тихий, глас 2» (2 pp.) | 232 | 95 |
| `svete_tihij_glas_3_znamennyj` | 09_3 «Свете тихий, глас 3» (2 pp.) | 235 | 95 |
| `svete_tihij_glas_6_znamennyj` | 09_4 «Свете тихий, глас 6» (2 pp.) | 283 | 94 |
| `bogoroditse_devo_znamennyj` | 16 «Богородице Дево, глас 4», «Из рукописи Кирилло-Белоезерского монастыря (632/889)» | 154 | 56 |

- **The notes** were read from the PDFs' vector shapes, as for 09_1:
  the shapes were measured in staff spaces (the four PDFs are drawn at
  different scales), filled heads with stems are quarters, hollow ones
  halves, wide hollow ones wholes, and a dot right of a head makes it
  dotted. The pitch comes from the head's position on its staff (treble
  clef, one flat in all four). There are no accidentals. All four go
  below the staff: tone 6 down to A3.
- **The slurs** are the source's, each joined to the notes nearest its two
  ends. In these files a slur does not always mark one syllable: some
  slurs carry two syllables (e.g. «с То-» in «Богородице Дево»), and long
  melismas often have no slur at all, only a row of hyphens in the text
  (e.g. «Плод» over a whole line).
- **The syllables** were placed under the notes nearest them, by their
  positions in the text layer (Church Slavonic in the UCS fonts
  Irmologion and Staro Uspenskaya; tone 6 partly in modern spelling). A
  syllable that came out on an inner note of a slur whose first note had
  none was moved to that first note (8 cases, in tones 2 and 6: e.g. «лнца», «мже»). The
  source's spacing is loose, so the four scores were compared with the
  source pages line by line, by eye.
- **The text** was converted to modern civil script the same way as
  09_1. The source's syllable division is kept (e.g. «бе-зсме-ртна-го»,
  «жен-на-го» in tone 2). The pre-reform spelling is kept («безсмертнаго»,
  «святаго»), and so is tone 6's «И-су-се». A word-final ъ is dropped.
  Where the source puts a closing consonant under the end of a melisma
  («свѣ́ — тъ», «по-е́ — мъ», «плодъ»), it joins the syllable
  («свет», «ем», «Плод»). Capitals follow the civil text of 09_1:
  sentence starts, and the divine names and pronouns («Отца», «Иисусе
  Христе», «Сына», «Святаго Духа», «Бога», «Сыне Божий», «Тя»; in
  «Богородице Дево» also «Благодатная», «Марие», «Господь», «с Тобою»,
  «Ты», «Плод», «Твоего», «Спаса»).
- **Editorial changes** (the source has an obvious gap):
  - tone 2, m11: the source has only «бо́ — й,» for «Божий». «жий,» was
    put on the last note, where the «й,» is printed.
  - tone 6, m1: the source has «свѧ — ѧ» for «святыя». «ты» was put on
    the slurred pair F–G at the end of the line, which has no syllable in
    the source.
  - tone 3: the titlo abbreviations «ѻ҆ц҃а» and «хрⷭ҇-сте» were written out
    as «О-тца» and «Хри-сте».
- **House style** as in 09_1: one measure per printed line with hidden
  time signatures, melisma lines on all multi-note syllables (words split
  after a melisma), lyrics in Arial 14 with minimum distance 1.3 (09_1's
  style file). The title text has the tone as its second line, which
  becomes the LilyPond subsubtitle. «Богородице Дево» has the source's
  manuscript note as a second composer line ("Белозерского", for the
  source's misspelling "Белоезерского").
- **Outputs:** `.musicxml`, `.ly` and `.pdf` built with
  `tools/rebuild-score.py znamenny/NAME`. The note check is skipped, as
  for 09_1.
- In tone 6 the breve of «й» in «Бо-жий» and «да-яй» is printed low, on
  the slur below. It is part of the text, not a musical sign.

## Transcription: `tsaryu_nebesnyj_znamennyj.mscz`

In this folder: «Царю Небесный», tone 6, Znamenny chant: the version sung on
the Wikipedia page (see above). It was transcribed on 2026-10-23 from three
frames of a YouTube video whose audio matches the Wikipedia recording
(<https://www.youtube.com/watch?v=QhkhmG1tO_w>, frames saved as
`~/Downloads/output_0001.png`, `_0008.png`, `_0014.png`, not kept here).
The edition shown in the video is unidentified ("Глас 6-й / Знаменный
распев", nine numbered phrases).

- Clean typeset images: the pitches were checked with a notehead reader,
  and the note values, slurs, tenutos and lyrics were read by eye. One
  measure per printed segment, the phrase numbers as rehearsal marks
  1–9, system breaks as printed, the final fermata.
- In phrase 5 the final consonant of «благих» is printed "(х)" under the
  last note; it is kept as a lyric there.
- MuseScore leaves out a few short melisma lines in its tight layout
  ("Со", "ще", "бла", "спа"); they are in the data, and the LilyPond PDF
  draws them.
- Outputs (`.musicxml`, `.ly`, `.pdf`) built with `tools/rebuild-score.py`.

**Compared with the Synodal Triodion (1899), p. 311** («По 50-м псалме,
стихира, глас 6»; see «Царю Небесный» above): the same melody. The
first two phrases were compared by eye, square note by square note, and
match in contour and grouping. Phrase 1: «Ца» two notes rising, «рю» a
higher pair, «не» a lower pair, «бе́с» four up and three down, «ный» on
the middle note. Phrase 2: «у» two rising, «тѣ́» a dotted note and a step
down, «ши» two rising, «те» one high, «лю» two falling. An automatic
comparison of the whole page was inconclusive, because the square-note
reader is thrown off by the mixed stem directions on this page. Later
phrases were not checked, and differences there remain possible.
