# Words split for a melisma ("sme ___ king"), for voice-explode-mscz.py.
#
# The house style splits a word after a melisma syllable, since MuseScore
# draws the melisma line only at a word's end. A voice that sings the split
# syllable on a single note has no melisma there, and the script joins the
# word again ("of-fer") if the split is listed here; the score itself can't
# tell "sme ___ king" from "U ___ roep" (two words).
#
# One split per line: the word's syllables up to the split | those after it,
# as in the score (hyphens, case and punctuation don't matter). A word split
# twice has two lines (ge|boor, boor|te).
