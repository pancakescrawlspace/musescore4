Why grieve, why pine away,
Why shed these tears?
I need only pray to God
And call upon His aid.

God! You are my hope, my joy in this life;
Soothe me, O God, in my bitter lot.

I shall lift my soul toward Him,
Raise my mind to the heavens,
Remain humble in His presence,
And speak with a sorrowful heart.

Troubles and afflictions are everywhere;
Everything around me brings fear.
Where can I hide from misfortune?
Where can I find a steadfast shield?

O God, in You lies all my peace;
With You, I can live even amidst adversity.
I turn to Him with a contrite heart;
My whole life is in His hands.
